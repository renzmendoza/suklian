How to use Suklian

  1. Add each member and type each contribution.
  2. Choose who paid first and enter the total they paid to the cashier.
  3. Enter the wallet cash available and let the app compute who gives or receives changes.
  4. Use the final summary button to copy the breakdown for your group chat.
